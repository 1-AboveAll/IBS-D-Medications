This repo compiles the medications (both approved and
off-label) used in the treatment of IBS-D (and some degree SIBO) and their
mechanisms of action. As someone who has suffered from IBS-D for a long time,
I've noticed a disorganization of information online, so I decided to compile
it. Feel free to contribute.
